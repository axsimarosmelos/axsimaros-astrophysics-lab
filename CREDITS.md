# Imagery and source credits

## Planetary surface maps

Sun, Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Saturn ring maps are by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**, distributed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**.

- Creator and texture collection: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Available texture archive: https://wp.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

These are visualization maps, including artistic interpretation and processing of imagery. They are mapped to 3D objects and combined with lighting, atmospheric effects, and color adjustments in this website; they are not presented as raw calibrated observations.

The original 2K JPEG files were obtained through these public mirrors:

- Jupiter: https://github.com/CarParty/CarParty
- Other Solar System Scope maps: https://github.com/igorSzymczak/AF2-Client

## Earth, night lights, and Moon

These files come from the **three.js r160 example asset collection**:

- `earth.jpg`: `examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg`
- `earth-night.png`: `examples/textures/planets/earth_lights_2048.png`
- `moon.jpg`: `examples/textures/planets/moon_1024.jpg`

Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160/examples/textures/planets

The source repository's MIT license is reproduced in [THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt](THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt). This site uses these example images, not the three.js JavaScript library. Day/night blending, illumination, atmosphere, and clouds are applied by the site's shaders.

## Hero artwork and rendered scenes

The Saturn homepage hero is an AI-generated artist's visualization created for Axsimaros, delivered as a WebP asset. It is not a NASA photograph. The black hole, nebula, galaxy, stars, and additional atmospheric details are rendered procedurally by the site's WebGL code. Model limitations appear in the interface and README.

## Learning and news

Library articles link to their NASA learning sources. NASA's black-hole visualization provides useful context for the illustrated bending and brightness effects: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13326/

Live headlines, timestamps, images when provided, and publisher links are retrieved from the Spaceflight News API: https://api.spaceflightnewsapi.net/v4/docs/

Fallback headlines link to their original publishers and are labeled as a dated selection. All publication names and associated article images belong to their respective owners. The site is independent of those organizations.
